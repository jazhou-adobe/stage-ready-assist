02 · Use Cases

Two ways to arrive stage-ready

Whichever door you walk through, the same rehearsal tools are waiting on the other side.

Already have a script

Paste it in and the teleprompter starts tracking your pace, filler words, and long pauses in real time. Download the take, re-listen to it, then copy a ready-made coaching prompt into ChatGPT or Claude for a deeper report — and go again.

Starting from nothing

Do a blind rehearsal with no script on screen — just say what's in your head. The take is captured and transcribed automatically. Copy a ready-made rewrite prompt to turn that raw transcript into a polished script, then drop it straight back into the teleprompter.

04 · Practice Anywhere

Four ways the tools meet you where you are

AIn the browser

The full teleprompter, webcam preview, and session recording — live score, pace, and filler-word tracking on every take.
BChrome extension

Opens the practice app in a narrow window docked to your browser, so you can rehearse beside whatever page you're already on. Mic, camera, and voice commands work exactly like the full app.
COn mobile

The script and teleprompter text scale down automatically on phone-width screens — rehearse on a commute or between sets at the gym.
DThe AI coaching loop

Every report page has a one-click prompt, pre-filled with your script and transcript, ready to paste into ChatGPT or Claude for a deeper coaching pass.
